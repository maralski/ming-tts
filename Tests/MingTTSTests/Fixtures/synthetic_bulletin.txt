Good evening, and welcome to the Harbourview Evening Digest for Thursday, November 6, 2026. It's 6:30 p.m., it's 17°C in the city, and here's what you need to know tonight.
First, the money. The Harbourview Index closed up 0.8% at 8,214.3 points, its best day in 3 weeks. Quillon Systems led the gains, jumping 18.4% to $27.65 after it lifted its full-year profit forecast to between $410M and $430M. The local dollar bought 64.2 US cents, and gold slipped 1.1% to US$3,982 an ounce. Economists at Fernway Capital now expect the central bank to cut rates by 0.25 percentage points in February, though they say a move in March is "just as likely."
In the city, council voted 7-2 last night to approve the $48.5 million ferry terminal rebuild. Mayor Ingrid Solberg called it "the biggest thing we've done for the waterfront since the 1980s." Work starts in mid-2027 and should finish by Dec. 2029; the old terminal, built in 1964, will stay open until then. Residents can read the plans at www.harbourview.example/ferry or email ferry@harbourview.example with questions before 5 p.m. on Dec. 12.
The weather bureau has issued a severe wind warning for the coast from 4 p.m. tomorrow. Gusts of up to 110 km/h are possible, with 25–40 mm of rain in the hills. Ferry services may be cancelled at short notice, so check the timetable before you travel. The weekend looks calmer: a top of 22° on Saturday and 24° on Sunday.
On the roads, the new 1.2 km Westhaven bridge opens to traffic at 6:00 a.m. Monday. Transport officials say it will cut 20 minutes off the morning commute for about 68,500 drivers a day. Buses on routes 12, 14 and 47 will be re-routed from the same day.
In health news, a trial at St. Luke's Hospital found a new asthma inhaler cut emergency visits by 31% among 860 patients aged 12 to 65. Dr. Simone Achterberg, who led the study, said the results were "encouraging, but not the end of the story." The full paper is published in this week's issue of the Northern Medical Review.
Sport now. The Northgate Rovers made it 7 straight home wins with a 3-1 victory over the Eastvale Comets on Saturday. Captain Sofia Brennan scored twice in the 2nd half, and coach Daniel Okafor said afterwards: "We're not getting carried away — there's a long way to go." In basketball, the Fernhill Falcons lost 98-101 in double overtime, their 4th loss in 5 games. And 19-year-old cyclist Tomás Vidal won stage 4 of the Coastal Tour, covering 182.5 km at an average of 38.7 km/h.
Finally, something lighter. The Harbourview Zoo has welcomed twin red panda cubs, weighing just 110 g and 125 g at birth. Keepers are asking the public to suggest names; entries close at midnight on Sunday, and the winner gets a family pass worth $189. That's the digest for tonight. I'm Marta Kowalczyk; thanks for listening, and we'll be back tomorrow at 7 a.m.
